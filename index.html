Who We Are

A charity built on
humility & heart

Amrix Forde was born in 2026 from a quiet but powerful conviction that true service never seeks the spotlight. We don't measure our worth by applause. We measure it by the number of plates filled, the number of children smiled at, and the number of lives reminded that they matter.

Rooted in community and guided by humility, we work on the ground: in orphanages, in neighbourhoods, and wherever hunger finds a face. We believe that when a community truly cares for its most vulnerable, it grows stronger for everyone.

Amrix Forde is not about grand gestures. It is about consistent, compassionate presence, showing up every week, every month, every year, without fail and without fanfare.

Our Mission

Feed the hungry,
one meal at a time

Every initiative we run is driven by one clear purpose: to reduce suffering and restore dignity, one person, one plate, one visit at a time.

🍛
Weekly Meal Drives

Every week, our volunteers head out to serve warm, nutritious meals to those in need. No one within our reach should go to bed hungry. That is our standing promise to our community.

🏠
Monthly Orphanage Visits

Each month, we visit orphanages, bringing food, essentials, and something just as important: presence. We spend time with children and remind them they are seen, valued, and loved.

🌱
Community of Care

Beyond meals and visits, we are building a lasting network of compassion, connecting those who want to give with those who need to receive, sustainably and with full integrity.
